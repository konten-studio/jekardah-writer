## Safe transformation

Source: `Tiga proposal gue ditolak. Di proposal keempat, gue memperjelas contoh
hasil kerja. Proposal itu diterima.`

Safe spine: repeated rejection -> one documented change -> fourth proposal
accepted. Unsafe lesson: `Klien selalu membeli bukti, bukan ide.` The source
supports one experience, not a universal buying law.

## Dialogue status

Source: `Intinya dia bilang harga kami terlalu mahal.`

Safe: `Dia bilang, kurang lebih, harga kami terlalu mahal.`

Unsafe: `Dia menatap gue dan berkata, "Harga kalian kelewat mahal."` This adds
verbatim wording, gaze, and scene detail.

## Motive and emotion

Source: `Setelah presentasi, partner gue tidak membalas chat selama dua hari.`

Safe: describe the silence and the author's reaction. Unsafe: `Dia malu karena
presentasi kami gagal.` The partner's inner state is unknown.

## Honest ending

Source: `Views membaik selama dua minggu, lalu turun lagi.`

Safe transformation: the experiment produced a temporary improvement and a
clearer question. Unsafe ending: `Akhirnya gue menemukan formula konten yang
berhasil.`
