# HEIA Content Check

## Contents

- Hook
- Empathy
- Information
- Action

Use HEIA as a completeness check, not a rigid four-section template.

## Hook

Earn attention with a supported tension, consequence, decision, or change. When
`hook-gokil` is active, pass it the story lock, transformation, and available
payoff rather than finalizing the hook here.

## Empathy

Show why this situation is recognizable to the intended audience. Use a shared
constraint, desire, risk, or frustration supported by the source or clearly
framed as audience context. Do not manufacture demographic pain.

## Information

Deliver the event, mechanism, evidence, choice, or lesson promised by the
opening. Prefer concrete supported detail over repeated explanation.

## Action

End with the next action appropriate to the piece: reconsider, try, compare,
reply, subscribe, buy, or simply hold the lesson. Preserve the user's CTA intent
and do not bolt a sales CTA onto a story that does not support it.
