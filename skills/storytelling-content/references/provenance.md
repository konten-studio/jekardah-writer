# Provenance And Design Influence

This skill is an original, compact operational distillation influenced by a
privately supplied Indonesian storytelling ebook. The source document is not
distributed, copied, or required at runtime.

High-level influences include a hook-empathy-information-action content check,
a five-beat story scaffold, causal expansion, scene detail, pacing, layered
information release, delivery practice, format and angle repurposing, and an
explicit honesty boundary. The wording, workflow, truth contract, composition
contract, examples, and repository integration in this skill are original.

Popular neuroscience explanations and marketing statistics from educational
material are intentionally excluded from the operating rules. The skill relies
on inspectable narrative practices and source fidelity rather than scientific
authority claims or performance guarantees.
