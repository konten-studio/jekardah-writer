# Delivery Notes

## Contents

- Annotation vocabulary
- Pace and pauses
- Emphasis and energy
- Boundaries

Keep source wording unchanged in `delivery-notes` mode. Add a compact legend,
then annotate only moments that benefit from direction.

- `[pause]`: allow a turn or consequence to land;
- `[slower]`: protect an important detail from being rushed;
- `[faster]`: compress setup or a repeated sequence;
- `[emphasize: words]`: mark the phrase carrying contrast or consequence;
- `[energy +1]` / `[energy -1]`: relative change, not theatrical instruction;
- `[tone: light|tense|reflective|direct]`: local delivery intent.

Match delivery to meaning. A serious voice does not automatically increase
stakes, and high energy does not fix a weak line. Avoid annotating every
sentence. Never add a pause that changes a qualifier's attachment or makes a
claim more absolute.
