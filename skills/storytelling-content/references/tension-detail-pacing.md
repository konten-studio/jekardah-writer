# Tension, Detail, And Pacing

## Contents

- Causal chains
- Scene zoom
- Emotional accuracy
- Tempo

## Causal chains

Expand `because of that` into the shortest supported chain that makes the change
understandable. Each link must answer: what changed, what choice followed, and
what consequence resulted? Delete links that merely repeat the same fact.

## Scene zoom

Zoom into moments where a decision, reversal, discovery, loss, or consequence
becomes visible. Use known objects, actions, words, physical reactions, and
constraints. Do not add weather, room details, gestures, or bodily sensations
merely to make the prose cinematic.

Replace abstract emotion with supported evidence:

- Weak: `Gue takut banget.`
- Strong only when true: `Gue baca email itu tiga kali sebelum berani balas.`

An observable action can suggest emotion, but do not claim it proves a specific
inner state.

## Tempo

- Slow down the decisive choice, reversal, or emotional consequence.
- Speed through logistics, repeated attempts, and connective chronology.
- Use shorter sentences for impact only when the content earns emphasis.
- Use paragraph breaks to mark turns, not after every sentence by habit.
- Preserve enough context that compression does not distort cause or scope.

Pacing is allocation of attention. It is not permission to omit inconvenient
facts or overstate a minor moment.
