# WTF and Killer Hook Types

## Core logic

The e-book models ordinary expectation as `if A, then B; if not A, then C`. A WTF hook creates a defensible reversal: `if A, then C` or `if not A, then B`. Use contradiction to reveal a real mechanism, not to state a false opposite.

WTF types 1-6 primarily reverse common logic. Killer types 7-15 primarily challenge beliefs, identities, patterns, or systems. The boundary is practical, not absolute.

## Emotional pillars

| Pillar | Audience impulse | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| Gain | `Gue pengin dapetin itu` | A supported reward, improvement, opportunity, or relief exists |
| Pain | `Gue harus hindarin itu` | A supported risk, mistake, loss, regret, or frustration exists |
| Curiosity | `Gue harus tahu kenapa` | The body contains a satisfying explanation or reveal |

## Fifteen types

| # | Type | Core formula | Best evidence/angle | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Good = Bad | `[Hal baik] malah bikin [akibat buruk]` | A convention has a real downside | False reversal |
| 02 | Crazy Math | `[Angka spesifik] yang bikin speechless` | Verified numbers or measured contrast | Invented/rounded statistics |
| 03 | Broken Label | `[Label] tapi [kenyataan kontradiktif]` | Identity and outcome genuinely conflict | Stereotyping or missing context |
| 04 | Silent Killer | `[Aktivitas normal] ternyata setara [bahaya besar]` | Evidence supports hidden harm or analogy | Medical/scientific exaggeration |
| 05 | Time Shock | `[Hasil besar] dalam [waktu absurd]` | Exact duration and result exist | Omitting conditions |
| 06 | Dark Horse | `[Background rendah] tapi [pencapaian tinggi]` | A documented underdog story | Belittling the subject |
| 07 | Forbidden Truth | `Gak ada yang berani bilang tapi [kebenaran]` | A defensible overlooked view | Fake consensus/secrecy |
| 08 | Reverse Reality | `Bukan [X] yang nentuin [Y], tapi [kebalikannya]` | Mechanism genuinely reverses intuition | Replacing nuance with a binary |
| 09 | Confession | `Gue akuin gue [kelemahan relatable]` | First-person source material | Inventing experience or vulnerability |
| 10 | System Glitch | `Kamu gak [gagal], sistemnya yang [rusak]` | Structural evidence is present | Removing personal agency without basis |
| 11 | Pattern Break | `[X], [X], [X], ternyata [kebalikan]` | A sequence contains a real turn | Empty wordplay |
| 12 | Absurd Comparison | `[X remeh] ternyata sama efektifnya kayak [Y diagungkan]` | Comparable evidence or a clearly framed metaphor | False equivalence |
| 13 | Future Shock | `Dalam [X tahun], [Y] bakal [perubahan drastis]` | Sourced forecast or attributed scenario | Presenting speculation as certainty |
| 14 | Identity Crisis | `Kamu pikir kamu [X positif], padahal [Y negatif]` | Behavior conflicts with self-image | Insulting or diagnosing the audience |
| 15 | Domino Effect | `1 [aksi kecil] yang trigger [efek berantai besar]` | The body traces the chain | Unsupported causation |

## Selection guidance

- Measured experiment: Crazy Math, Time Shock, Pattern Break, Domino Effect.
- Anecdote: Confession, Pattern Break, cautious Good = Bad, curiosity-led Time Shock.
- Strong sourced opinion: Forbidden Truth, Reverse Reality, System Glitch.
- Personal transformation: Dark Horse, Broken Label, Confession.
- Hidden downside: Good = Bad or Silent Killer only when proof supports the harm.
- Forecast: Future Shock only with attribution and uncertainty matching the source.
- Thin context: choose a curiosity hook or ask for evidence; do not force a high-claim type.

Prefer the type that exposes the source's strongest true tension. Do not fill every formula mechanically.
